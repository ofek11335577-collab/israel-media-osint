\# OSINT Global Desk | Tactical Intelligence Terminal



A professional, real-time OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) intelligence dashboard designed to automatically aggregate, categorize, and display live news and security reports across multiple global zones (Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Yemen, Syria, Iraq, Gaza/West Bank, and US/Global).



\## 🚀 Key Features

\- \*\*Live RSS Ingestion Pipeline:\*\* Automated parsing engine (`fetcher.py`) pulling verified updates directly from major international news feeds (Reuters, BBC, Al Jazeera).

\- \*\*Real-Time Chronological Sorting:\*\* Displays the freshest intelligence reports instantly at the top of the terminal based on true publication timestamps (`pubDate` parsing).

\- \*\*Tactical Newsroom UI:\*\* Dark-mode terminal design featuring a live news ticker, zone navigation flags, lead story high-priority cards, and full article view.

\- \*\*Analytics Terminal:\*\* Searchable and sortable data archive view for advanced analyst inspection.



\## 🛠️ Tech Stack

\- \*\*Backend \& Pipeline:\*\* Python, SQLite, XML Parsers, Email.utils (RFC 2822 date parsing).

\- \*\*Frontend \& UI:\*\* Streamlit Cloud, Custom HTML/CSS (Tactical Terminal Theme).
